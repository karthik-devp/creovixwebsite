Scale Your Support Without
Scaling Your Headcount

Growing SaaS companies face a common challenge: as the product improves and the user base expands, the volume of customer questions grows too. Building an in-house support team takes time and resource.

Creovix provides dedicated support agents who work inside your tools, follow your processes and represent your brand — without requiring you to manage recruitment, training and HR for each hire.

We handle the day-to-day customer communication so your product and engineering teams can focus on building.

  • SOP-driven responses for consistency
  • Works within your existing helpdesk

What We Handle for SaaS Companies

Our agents are trained on your product documentation and support processes before handling customer conversations.

Product Questions

General questions about how your software works.

Account Support

Login issues, account settings and subscription queries.

Onboarding Assistance

Helping new users get started with your product.

Email Support

Professional written responses across your support inbox.

Live Chat

Real-time help through your chat platform.

Helpdesk Management

Ticket triage, status updates and queue management.

Customer-Success Assistance

Proactive check-ins and customer touchpoints.

First-Level Issue Triage

Identifying and routing issues before escalation.

Note: Creovix does not provide technical engineering support or code-level debugging. Our agents handle customer communication and first-level triage only.
