Our Core Services

Email Support

Our agents manage your entire email support queue, responding to customer enquiries, processing requests and following up — all in your brand voice.

Live Chat Support

Fast, professional responses to live chat enquiries through your existing chat platform. Agents follow your brand guidelines and escalation paths.

What's Included

  • Real-time responses
  • Chat queue management

  • Handoff protocols

Customer Service

Day-to-day customer service management across your chosen channels. We maintain your brand standards and keep customer satisfaction at the centre of every interaction.

What's Included

  • Multi-channel handling
  • Brand voice alignment
  • Issue resolution
  • Customer follow-up

Helpdesk Support

We work directly inside your helpdesk system — managing tickets, updating statuses and ensuring nothing falls through the cracks.

What's Included

  • Ticket triage
  • Status management
  • Internal notes
  • SLA tracking

Back-Office Support

Selected operational tasks that sit alongside customer support — from data entry and order management to internal admin that keeps your support workflow running.

What's Included

  • Data entry
  • Order processing assistance
  • Internal coordination
  • Reporting support

Overflow Support

When ticket volume spikes — around product launches, promotions or seasonal peaks — our overflow support keeps response times consistent.

What's Included

  • On-demand capacity
  • Rapid deployment
  • Volume management
  • Seamless integration

Logistics Support

Our Approach

Dedicated, Not Shared

Our agents are dedicated to your account during their working hours. They learn your product, your tone and your processes — not rotated across unrelated clients. This means consistent quality, faster ramp-up and genuine familiarity with your customers.

  • Agents aligned to your brand and standards
  • SOP-driven consistency across every ticket
  • Regular QA and performance reviews
  • Flexible team sizing as you grow
