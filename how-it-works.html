The Process

How We Get Started

A structured onboarding process that minimises disruption and ensures alignment from day one.

1
Week 1
Discovery Call

We start with a detailed consultation call to understand your business, your customers, your support volumes and your current challenges. There are no scripts or assumptions — just a genuine conversation about what you need.

What gets discussed
  • Support volume & channels
  • Current tools & helpdesk
  • Common customer issues
  • SLA expectations
  • Brand voice & tone
2
Weeks 1–2
Proposal & Alignment

Based on the discovery call, we put together a clear proposal outlining the team structure, service scope and onboarding timeline. We answer any questions and refine the scope until you're confident it meets your needs.

What's included in the proposal
  • Team structure
  • Service scope
  • Onboarding timeline
  • Pricing & terms
3
Weeks 2–3
SOP Development & Agent Training

Before any agent handles a customer conversation, we document your processes. We build standard operating procedures based on your existing knowledge base, support history and brand guidelines. Agents are trained and tested before going live.

SOP setup includes
  • Process documentation
  • Knowledge base review
  • Brand voice alignment
  • Agent training & testing
  • QA sign-off
4
Week 4
Go Live & Ongoing

Your dedicated support team starts handling customer conversations inside your existing tools. We monitor closely in the early weeks, hold regular check-ins and make refinements as needed. This is a long-term partnership, not a one-off setup.

Ongoing commitments
  • Regular performance reviews
  • QA monitoring
  • Reporting
  • Scaling as needed
  • Direct communication
How We Operate

The Principles Behind Our Work

The principles that guide how we work with every client.

Dedicated, Not Shared

Your agents work on your account during their working hours. Consistency comes from familiarity — the same people, learning your product and customers over time.

Process-First

Every interaction follows a documented SOP. Quality comes from clear processes, not luck. We build the playbook before a single conversation is handled.

Transparent Communication

Regular reporting and open communication. You always know how your support team is performing — no black boxes, no guesswork.

Built to Scale

Start with the team size you need today and scale as your business grows. Our model is flexible — add capacity when you need it, without starting from scratch.

FAQ

Common Questions About Onboarding

How long does onboarding take?
Typically 2–4 weeks depending on the complexity of your processes and the size of your documentation requirements. Simpler setups can be faster.
What do I need to prepare?
Ideally, existing support documentation, FAQs and access to your helpdesk. We can work with whatever you have and help structure what's missing.
Will the same agents handle my account every day?
Yes. We aim for consistency. The same core team handles your account so agents develop familiarity with your product and customers over time.
How do you handle quality assurance?
We conduct regular QA reviews of tickets and conversations, provide reporting and hold periodic review calls to ensure standards are maintained.
What if things need adjusting after go-live?
Refinements are expected and welcome. We hold regular check-ins especially in the first few weeks to make adjustments quickly.
