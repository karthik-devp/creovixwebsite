Why Creovix for Logistics

Support That Keeps Shipments Moving

In freight forwarding, 3PL, and supply chain operations, speed and accuracy in communication are paramount. Uncommunicated carrier delays, missed delivery exceptions, and unanswered tracking inquiries directly cause shipper frustration and costly operational friction.

Creovix embeds dedicated, trained support agents into your existing systems to handle daily tracking inquiries, carrier escalations, proof-of-delivery (POD) requests, and return routing.

We operate as a true extension of your operations team, following strict standard operating procedures (SOPs) so your freight coordinators and dispatchers can focus on route planning, fleet efficiency, and customer acquisition.

Dedicated agents trained on your specific transportation & dispatch workflows.
Proactive exception handling that alerts customers before delays turn into complaints.
Seamless system integration inside your existing TMS, ticketing, or CRM software.
Flexible scaling to support peak shipping periods, freight surges, and quarterly rushes.

Scope of Service

What We Handle for Logistics Companies

Our agents are trained on your operating procedures and logistics terminology before taking on live shipments.

Tracking & Status Updates

Real-time delivery progress updates, milestone tracking, and estimated delivery dates communicated clearly to shippers and consignees.

Carrier Coordination

Direct communication with freight carriers, LTL couriers, and 3PL partners to follow up on stuck cargo, customs holds, and line-haul schedules.

Exception Management

Triage and resolve delivery exceptions — wrong addresses, missed delivery windows, gate code requests, and weather delay notices.

Proof of Delivery (POD)

Retrieving, verifying, and distributing signed bills of lading (BOL), recipient signatures, and photo proofs of delivery to clients.

Reverse Logistics & Returns

Processing return authorizations (RMA), issuing return labels, tracking return transits, and syncing with fulfillment hubs.

Warehouse & Dispatch Sync

Internal query routing with fulfillment centers, inventory checks, pick/pack confirmations, and dispatch scheduling.

Discrepancy & Claims Intake

Documenting lost, stolen, or damaged shipment reports, collecting photos, and gathering paperwork for carrier claims processing.

Omnichannel Support Queue

Managing support queues across email, live chat, ticketing platforms, and WhatsApp/SMS within agreed response SLAs.
